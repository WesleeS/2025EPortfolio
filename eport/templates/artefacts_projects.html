Assorted Projects

Game Programming

Both in highschool and college, I took classes for game development in both Unity and Godot -- utilizing C# and GDScript respectively. I have no remaining hosted projects from my highschool days (I'm sure I have some project files on an ExHDD), but my current college ones are readily hosted! My linked Itch page contains links to them, which primarily feature 2D games along with a multiplayer proof-of-concept. In highschool, I notably made a VR shooting range game, and even produced amateur grade animations of Shakespeare's plays.

Outside of class work, I also have played around with the late BYOND engine, and various MUD frameworks such as Evennia and LambdaMOO. Text games have always been a measure amusing to me for the depth in creativity and accessibility. By far, text games feature the strongest ability to be accessible from the perspective of language barriers, vision accessibility (e.g., screen readers using TTS), and even development cost. These types of games made a small resurgence with releases such as "Caves of Qud" in the simplified ascii style, or even the steam release of Dwarf Fortress, but true text-based games lag behind unfortunately.

CineFiles

Produced as a group project, I helped develop a small movie review site that utilized the IMDb API to populate a selection of released movies. Users could then post reviews, comment on them, and we even had the support of the Django framework to produce an Admin panel to review posts for content. A simple implementation, but I remain decently proud of the results. This was one of the first times I worked in a group for a coding project too, and the results were quite alright!

StudyBuddy

Another webapp designed in a group was a note taking and sharing app, that had quite robust archive functions to store notes after a semester and allowed teachers to store notes and review the content within for the overall quality and coverage. More notably, this project had me leading a group of 10 members and the coordination of work to create a minimum viable product within the span of a few weeks in AGILE-like development.

Responsive Bots

In the same vein of text-based games, I also developed a Discord bot in Python with the Red-Bot framework. It was a great aid in the Covid years where I had played TTRPG's over the internet; and was made to feature sheet management, dice rolling, and a webpage companion to build characters within the FATE system. It saw use for a couple years, but was eventually shut down for a lack of interest in continued hosting of the AWS instance.
