Mansfield Center Internship 2025

Over a period in the summer of 2025, I worked as an Office Assistant at the Mansfield Center. This came after government cuts to funding which resulted in substantial staff loss. As a result, I wore many hats and had to help multiple program managers and our in-house IT member.

Completed administrative duties to assist supervisor, including catering for hosting an international summit and helping with materials for a Mansfield Foundation board meeting. Further IT specific work included the maintainance of a NeonOrangeCRM website for international SEA applicants for the YSEALI program and the conversion of old software data to new infrastructure.

  • Wrote software to parse email collisions from AmazonCognito and NeonOrangeCRM, covering 500+ emails.
  • Assisted office members with remote VPN setups.
  • Performed image processing for secure storage of 50+ applicants and their information.
  • Reviewed 800+ applications for YSEALI 2025 and 2026.
  • Created an 11 minute memento slideshow of 2025 YSEALI members.
